ANNUAL FINANCIAL SUMMARY - FISCAL YEAR 2024
============================================

Prepared by: Finance Division
Distribution: Executive Leadership Team, Board of Directors
Classification: Internal - Confidential
Report Date: January 30, 2025

DISCLAIMER
----------

This financial summary is prepared for internal management purposes
and has not been audited by an external accounting firm. The figures
presented are based on management accounts and may differ from the
final audited financial statements. All amounts are in US dollars
unless otherwise stated.

EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
------------------

Fiscal year 2024 was a transformative year for the company financially,
with strong revenue growth, improving unit economics, and a path to
sustainable profitability becoming increasingly clear. The investment
thesis -- that the combination of a differentiated product platform
with AI capabilities, a growing cloud business, and disciplined
execution would produce attractive financial returns -- was validated
by the year's results.

The company crossed several important financial milestones during the
year, including exceeding $15M in ARR, achieving positive free cash
flow for three consecutive quarters, and demonstrating operating
leverage as revenue growth outpaced operating expense growth for the
first time in the company's history.

COST OF REVENUE AND GROSS MARGIN
----------------------------------

Total cost of revenue: $5.28M
Gross profit: $14.52M
Gross margin: 73.3% (up from 70.1% in FY23)

Cost of revenue components:
- Cloud infrastructure: $2.1M (40%)
- Professional services delivery: $1.6M (30%)
- Customer support: $0.8M (15%)
- Third-party software licenses: $0.5M (10%)
- Other: $0.28M (5%)

The gross margin improvement of 320 basis points year-over-year was
driven by:
1. Cloud infrastructure optimization (14% cost per unit reduction)
2. Favorable revenue mix shift (more software, less services)
3. Professional services margin improvement (32% -> 37%)
4. Scale economies in customer support
