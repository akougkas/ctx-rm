QUARTERLY SALES REPORT - Q4 FISCAL YEAR 2024
============================================

Prepared by: Revenue Analytics Division
Distribution: Executive Leadership Team, Board of Directors
Classification: Internal - Confidential
Report Date: January 15, 2025

EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
------------------

The fourth quarter delivered outstanding results across all dimensions,
closing out fiscal year 2024 with strong momentum heading into the new
year. The combination of Q3 deal push-outs converting to closed business,
the federal fiscal year-end surge, enterprise budget flush activity, and
the continuing strength of our AI capabilities created a quarter that
exceeded even our most optimistic scenarios. The sales team executed at
the highest level we have seen in the company's history.

Every major product line contributed meaningfully to the result, and
every region met or exceeded their respective targets. The enterprise
segment had a record-breaking quarter with the largest single deal in
company history, while the cloud business maintained its growth
trajectory. Developer tools rounded out its recovery with solid
subscriber growth, and professional services delivered its highest
utilization and margin quarter ever.

The annual customer conference in Q3 generated significant Q4 pipeline,
and the team converted at a rate substantially above historical norms.
The competitive environment, while still challenging, worked in our
favor as competitor missteps and our strong product momentum created
several displacement opportunities that the team executed on
effectively.

MARKET ENVIRONMENT
------------------

Q4 marked a turning point in the broader economic cycle, with the
Federal Reserve announcing its first rate cut in September and
signaling a trajectory of further easing. This shift reinvigorated
business investment sentiment, particularly among mid-market companies
that had been deferring purchases. Enterprise CIOs reported increased
budget certainty for calendar year 2025, with AI and cloud remaining
top investment priorities.

The holiday season created the typical end-of-year procurement rush,
with procurement departments rushing to deploy remaining budgets
before fiscal year close. This dynamic was amplified by the
improved economic outlook, as companies that had been holding back
felt comfortable committing to multi-year investments. The result
was a condensed but highly productive selling period in November
and December.

Technology M&A activity accelerated in Q4, with several transactions
directly impacting our competitive landscape. Most notably, our
primary mid-market competitor was acquired by a private equity firm,
creating uncertainty among their customer base and a significant
opportunity for our team to engage with customers evaluating
alternatives. The acquisition created immediate pipeline of $2.8M
as concerned customers initiated evaluations.

SALES PERFORMANCE DETAIL
-------------------------

Enterprise Platform (EP):
The EP segment delivered a historic quarter with $3.35M in bookings.
The Q3 push-out deals all converted as expected, and the federal
year-end created exceptional demand. The largest deal in company
history -- a $1.8M multi-year platform agreement with a major
financial institution -- closed in early December after a 9-month
sales cycle. Average deal size jumped to $168K, reflecting both the
strength of our platform positioning and the bundled AI capabilities.

Cloud Services (CS):
Cloud revenue growth reaccelerated to 31% year-over-year as the
consumption optimization headwinds from Q3 stabilized. New workload
types, particularly AI inference and data analytics, are driving
increased consumption. The migration program continued with 32
enterprise accounts completing their move to cloud during Q4.
Consumption-based revenue represented 55% of cloud bookings, with
the balance in committed cloud contracts.

Developer Tools (DT):
DT achieved its strongest quarter with paid subscriber growth of
28% quarter-over-quarter. The free tier restructuring implemented
in Q3 delivered the expected improvement in conversion rates, which
reached 5.5% (up from 4.8%). The plugin marketplace now hosts 38
integrations and generated meaningful referral revenue. Competition
from the funded startup appears to have peaked, as their rapid
growth consumed significant burn rate with reports of internal
challenges.

Professional Services (PS):
PS achieved record utilization of 89% and delivered revenue above
plan. The team completed 12 enterprise implementation projects,
including the complex financial institution deployment. Customer
satisfaction scores reached 4.8/5.0. The expanded consultant base
from earlier in the year is now fully productive and contributing
to a healthier margin profile.

FINANCIAL SUMMARY
-----------------

The fourth quarter revenue closed at $6.7M, a record for any
single quarter in the company's history. This represents 34%
year-over-year growth compared to Q4 of the prior fiscal year,
and a 76% sequential increase over Q3, reflecting both the
seasonal strength of Q4 and the exceptional execution by the
team. The full fiscal year revenue totaled $19.8M, coming in
4% above the annual plan.

Gross margin for Q4 was 74.2%, the highest of the year, driven by
the favorable revenue mix with a high proportion of software
licensing and cloud commitments. The expanded scale of the cloud
infrastructure contributed to improved unit economics. Services
margin was 39%, also a record, reflecting the matured consultant
workforce and efficient project execution.

ARR exiting Q4 was $15.2M, representing a 28% year-over-year growth
rate. The recurring revenue mix reached 74% of total revenue for the
quarter. Deferred revenue grew 32%, providing strong visibility into
the first half of the next fiscal year.

Free cash flow was positive for the third consecutive quarter,
reaching $1.1M, driven by strong collections and the working
capital benefit of upfront annual payments on multi-year contracts.

REGIONAL PERFORMANCE
--------------------

North America:
Revenue of $4.02M, representing 60% of total and a record for the
region. The US market delivered exceptional performance across all
verticals, with financial services and federal being the standout
segments. The largest deal in company history was a US-based
financial institution. Canada contributed $245K, its strongest
quarter ever. The federal sales team, which started in Q3, closed
$620K in government deals, validating the investment in dedicated
public sector coverage.

EMEA:
Revenue of $1.54M, representing 23% of total. The region bounced
back strongly from the seasonal softness of Q3. The UK led with
$485K, including a major deal with a London-based fintech company.
Germany contributed $345K with a notable win in the automotive
sector. The competitive displacement opportunity from the PE
acquisition of our competitor was particularly pronounced in
EMEA, where the acquired company had significant market presence.

APAC:
Revenue of $804K, representing 12% of total and the region's best
quarter. Japan contributed $310K with two major enterprise deals
closing. Australia delivered $265K with strength in the financial
services and mining sectors. The Singapore data center, operational
since Q3, enabled the team to win deals that required in-region
data residency, contributing $115K in new business.

Latin America:
Revenue of $330K, representing 5% of total. Brazil had its
strongest quarter at $200K, driven by an enterprise deal in the
financial sector. Mexico contributed $65K and is establishing itself
as the region's second-largest market. The channel partner network
expanded to 8 active partners across the region.

FISCAL YEAR 2025 OUTLOOK
--------------------------

Based on the current pipeline, bookings trends, and market dynamics,
the preliminary FY25 target is $26.5M, representing 34% growth over
FY24. Key growth drivers include:
- AI platform expansion (expected to contribute $4M+ in new ARR)
- International expansion with dedicated regional leaders
- Competitive displacement from the PE-acquired competitor
- Federal/public sector ramp-up
- Partner ecosystem scaling

The FY25 plan will be finalized during the January board meeting
and communicated to the broader organization in February.
