ANNUAL SHIPPING AND LOGISTICS REPORT - FISCAL YEAR 2024
========================================================

Prepared by: Logistics and Distribution Division
Distribution: Executive Leadership, Operations Team
Classification: Internal - Confidential
Report Date: January 22, 2025

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
-----------------

The shipping and logistics function delivered strong performance during
fiscal year 2024, maintaining high service levels despite ongoing
challenges in the global freight market. The team successfully managed
the transition to a new carrier mix, implemented last-mile delivery
improvements, and deployed real-time shipment tracking that significantly
enhanced customer visibility into their orders.

Overall shipping volume increased 18% year-over-year, driven by the
company's revenue growth and geographic expansion. The logistics team
handled this growth while reducing per-shipment costs by 7%, a
testament to the negotiation leverage gained through volume commitments
and the operational efficiencies from routing optimization.

DELIVERY PERFORMANCE METRICS
------------------------------

The core delivery performance metrics for fiscal year 2024 show strong
results across most dimensions. On-time delivery rates improved for the
third consecutive year, and the team successfully reduced the average
shipping time through a combination of network optimization and carrier
partnership improvements.

On-time delivery rate: 94.7% (up from 93.2%)
Average delivery time: 3.2 days (down from 3.6 days)
Next-day delivery success: 98.2% (for express shipments)
Same-day delivery success: 95.8% (select metro areas)

The average shipping time of 3.2 days represents a meaningful
improvement that was achieved through several initiatives including
carrier route optimization, strategic inventory positioning closer
to major customer clusters, and the introduction of a predictive
shipping algorithm that pre-positions packages based on anticipated
demand patterns.

Regional delivery performance:
- North America domestic: 2.8 days average
- EMEA: 3.5 days average
- APAC: 4.8 days average
- Latin America: 5.2 days average

INTERNATIONAL SHIPPING
-----------------------

International shipping volumes grew 24% year-over-year, outpacing
domestic growth of 15%. Key developments in international shipping:

Customs compliance rate: 99.1% (zero shipments held for non-compliance)
Average customs clearance time: 1.4 days (down from 2.1 days)
Duty/tax accuracy rate: 98.7%

The deployment of our customs management system in Q1 significantly
improved international shipping operations by automating harmonized
tariff code classification, pre-calculating duties and taxes, and
generating compliant documentation. This reduced customs-related
delays by 45% and virtually eliminated clearance issues.

LAST-MILE DELIVERY INNOVATIONS
---------------------------------

Several last-mile delivery innovations were piloted or deployed:

1. Locker delivery partnerships: Established agreements with package
   locker networks in 8 metropolitan areas, providing customers with
   convenient pickup options. Adoption reached 12% of eligible
   deliveries by Q4.

2. Weekend delivery: Expanded Saturday delivery service to 15
   additional markets, bringing the total to 42 markets with
   weekend service.

3. Delivery appointment scheduling: Launched the ability for
   customers to schedule specific delivery windows. Currently
   available in 20 markets with plans for broader rollout.

4. Green delivery options: Introduced carbon-neutral shipping as
   an option, powered by carrier offset programs. Selected by
   8% of customers who were presented with the option.

APPENDIX B: DETAILED CARRIER PERFORMANCE
------------------------------------------

UPS Performance Analysis:
Total shipments via UPS: 18,500
On-time rate: 93.8%
Average transit time: 3.1 days
Damage claim rate: 0.42%
Cost per shipment: $68.20
Customer complaints: 145

UPS performed well for ground shipments in the eastern United States,
with particularly strong performance in the Northeast corridor and
Midwest regions. Performance degraded somewhat during peak season
(November-December) when network congestion led to an increase in
late deliveries. The dedicated account team was responsive to
escalations and resolved most issues within 48 hours. The new
Saturday delivery service through UPS showed strong adoption with
reliable service levels. Cost increases were moderate at 3.5%
year-over-year, in line with the published rate guide adjustments.

FedEx Performance Analysis:
Total shipments via FedEx: 16,200
On-time rate: 95.2%
Average transit time: 2.9 days
Damage claim rate: 0.38%
Cost per shipment: $72.40
Customer complaints: 112

FedEx maintained its position as the premium carrier option, with
strong performance across both ground and express services. The
FedEx Express service was particularly reliable for time-sensitive
shipments, with a 98.2% on-time rate for next-day deliveries.
International shipments through FedEx International were consistent
but slightly more expensive than alternatives. The integration of
FedEx tracking data into our customer portal was completed in Q2,
providing real-time visibility. FedEx announced a 5.9% general
rate increase effective January 2025, which we are negotiating
against with volume commitment leverage.

DHL International Analysis:
Total shipments via DHL: 8,600
On-time rate: 91.5%
Average transit time: 4.2 days (international)
Damage claim rate: 0.52%
Cost per shipment: $95.80
Customer complaints: 98

DHL emerged as our strongest international carrier, with particular
excellence on Asia-Pacific and European routes. The expansion of
DHL's share from 40% to 50% of international volume was justified
by a 15% improvement in APAC transit times and the opening of their
new Singapore hub that aligns with our distribution center. Customs
clearance support from DHL was industry-leading, with their
electronic pre-clearance system reducing customs delays by 40%.

Regional Carriers Analysis:
Total shipments via regional carriers: 4,500
On-time rate: 92.3%
Average transit time: 2.4 days
Damage claim rate: 0.28%
Cost per shipment: $52.10
Customer complaints: 67

The introduction of regional carriers for last-mile delivery in
select markets proved successful, offering lower costs and faster
delivery for short-haul shipments. OnTrac (West Coast), LSO
(Southwest), and Spee-Dee (Midwest) provided reliable service
with competitive pricing. The lower damage rate reflects the
reduced number of handoffs in the delivery chain.

Internal fleet operations:

Fleet statistics:
- Vehicles: 8 (5 vans, 3 box trucks)
- Total miles driven: 48,000
- Deliveries completed: 1,200
- Average delivery cost: $45 (below carrier rate of $65)
- On-time rate: 97.5%

The fleet operation is evaluated annually for cost-effectiveness
against outsourcing to carriers. The current analysis supports
continued operation due to the white-glove service premium that
enterprise customers value and the flexibility it provides for
same-day delivery requests.

Cross-dock operations:
Two cross-dock facilities supplement the warehouse network:
- Newark, NJ: Handles 18% of East Coast volume
- Dallas, TX: Handles 12% of Central US volume

Cross-dock facilities enable rapid redistribution of inventory
between warehouses and carriers without long-term storage. Average
cross-dock throughput time is 4 hours, enabling next-day delivery
for time-sensitive shipments that would otherwise require 2-3 days.
