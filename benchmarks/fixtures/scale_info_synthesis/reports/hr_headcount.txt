ANNUAL HUMAN RESOURCES AND HEADCOUNT REPORT - FISCAL YEAR 2024
================================================================

Prepared by: Human Resources Division

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
-----------------

Fiscal year 2024 was a year of strategic talent investment for the
organization. The HR function supported the company's growth
objectives by executing a carefully planned hiring program, enhancing
employee development pathways, improving retention through targeted
compensation adjustments, and strengthening the company culture
during a period of significant growth and organizational change.

The labor market environment remained competitive for technology
talent throughout the year, though conditions moderated somewhat
compared to the extreme tightness of prior years. The shift toward
AI-related skills created new competitive dynamics in recruitment,
as companies across all sectors aggressively recruited engineers
and data scientists with AI/ML experience. Our ability to attract
this talent was aided by our authentic AI product story and the
opportunity for candidates to work on real AI systems at scale.

ORGANIZATIONAL OVERVIEW
------------------------

The company is organized into five primary divisions:
1. Engineering (including R&D, Platform, and AI)
2. Sales and Marketing
3. Customer Success and Support
4. Operations (including Supply Chain, Logistics, and IT)
5. General & Administrative (Finance, Legal, HR)

Each division is led by a VP or SVP who reports to the CEO. The
organizational structure remained stable during FY24, with one
notable change: the creation of a dedicated AI Engineering group
within the Engineering division, led by a newly hired VP of AI.

HEADCOUNT SUMMARY
------------------

The organization's total headcount at fiscal year end reflects the
deliberate hiring strategy executed throughout the year, balanced
against natural attrition and targeted organizational adjustments.

As of fiscal year end (December 31, 2024), the company employs
247 employees across all divisions and geographies. This represents
a net increase of 32 employees during the fiscal year, from a
starting headcount of 215 at the beginning of FY24. The growth
rate of 14.9% is aligned with the company's revenue growth rate
and reflects investments in engineering capacity (particularly
