QUARTERLY SALES REPORT - Q2 FISCAL YEAR 2024
============================================

Prepared by: Revenue Analytics Division

EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
------------------

The second quarter represented a significant step forward for the
organization, with momentum building across all major product lines
and geographies. The enterprise segment continued to perform above
expectations, while the cloud business crossed an important milestone
with consumption revenue exceeding licensed revenue for the first time.
Customer engagement metrics improved across the board, and the sales
pipeline entering Q3 is the strongest it has been in two years.

Several strategic initiatives launched in Q1 began to bear fruit in
Q2, most notably the AI feature suite which was released in early May
and generated substantial interest from both existing customers and
prospects. Early adoption metrics exceeded internal forecasts by 40%,
and the AI capabilities were cited as a differentiator in 23 competitive
evaluations during the quarter.

The competitive landscape continued to evolve with our primary mid-
market competitor completing their leadership transition and launching
an aggressive pricing campaign. We responded with targeted value-
messaging and a limited-time migration incentive that proved effective
in defending our installed base while winning several competitive
displacement deals.

MARKET ENVIRONMENT
------------------

Technology spending showed further improvement in Q2, with enterprise
IT budgets generally holding steady or increasing modestly. The AI
investment trend accelerated significantly, with CIOs reporting that
AI-related spending would represent 15-20% of their total IT budgets
by year end, up from less than 5% twelve months ago. This trend
directly benefits our product positioning given the AI capabilities
integrated into our platform.

Federal Reserve policy remained restrictive, but the market's forward-
looking expectations shifted toward potential rate cuts in Q3 or Q4,
which improved business confidence, particularly among mid-market
companies that had been deferring capital expenditures. IPO activity
resumed at a modest pace, with several of our late-stage startup
customers successfully going public during the quarter.

Industry consolidation continued with three notable M&A transactions
in our broader competitive ecosystem. None directly impacted our
addressable market, but the integration challenges faced by two of
the acquiring companies created opportunities for us to engage with
dissatisfied customers of the merged entities.

SALES PERFORMANCE DETAIL
-------------------------

Enterprise Platform (EP):
The EP segment delivered another strong quarter with several marquee
deals closing. The average enterprise deal size grew to $145K, up from
$128K in Q1, driven by broader platform adoption within accounts and
the bundling of AI capabilities with core platform licenses. Multi-
year commitment rates remained elevated at 47%, providing continued
revenue visibility. The land-and-expand motion showed particular
strength, with 35 existing enterprise accounts increasing their
contract value during the quarter.

Cloud Services (CS):
Cloud revenue growth accelerated to 34% year-over-year, up from 28%
in Q1. The consumption model continued to gain traction, with total
consumption revenue surpassing licensed revenue for the first time.
Customer workloads are diversifying beyond core compute to include
analytics, integration, and the new AI inference capabilities. The
cloud team successfully migrated 28 additional enterprise accounts
from on-premise deployments, bringing the total migration count to
a significant milestone.

Developer Tools (DT):
The DT segment showed marked improvement, recovering from the
competitive pressure experienced in Q1. The integrated development
environment plugin surpassed 150,000 downloads, a 3x increase from
the Q1 figure, and paid conversions improved to 4.8% from 3.2%.
Third-party integrations launched during Q2 with popular CI/CD
platforms, which expanded the addressable market and generated
meaningful inbound leads. Customer feedback on the developer
experience has been overwhelmingly positive.

Professional Services (PS):
PS revenue rebounded strongly as delayed Q1 projects commenced and
several new large-scale implementations began. Utilization rates
improved to 84%, exceeding the 82% target for the first time in
three quarters. The newly hired consultants from Q1 completed their
training programs and began billing at a higher rate than anticipated.
Customer satisfaction scores for PS engagements reached an all-time
high of 4.7 out of 5.0.

FINANCIAL SUMMARY
-----------------

Total Q2 revenue reached $5.1M, representing a strong sequential
increase and exceeding the internal forecast. This performance puts
us on a solid trajectory for the full year, with the first half
representing approximately 47% of the annual target. The revenue
mix continued its positive shift, with recurring revenue (ARR)
growing to 71% of total, up from 68% in Q1. Subscription revenue
specifically grew 26% year-over-year.

Gross margin for Q2 was 73.1%, up from 72.4% in Q1 and 70.6% in the
year-ago quarter. The improvement was driven by cloud infrastructure
optimization efforts that reduced per-unit serving costs by 14%, plus
the continuing shift toward higher-margin software revenue. Services
margin improved to 36% as utilization rates recovered and the new
consultants became productive.

Operating expenses grew 8% year-over-year, reflecting planned
investments in R&D (particularly the AI team) and sales capacity.
However, operating leverage improved as revenue growth outpaced
expense growth, with the operating margin expanding by 240 basis
points compared to the year-ago period.

REGIONAL PERFORMANCE
--------------------

North America:
Revenue of $3.06M, representing 60% of total. The US market continued
its leadership with strength across financial services, healthcare,
technology, and retail verticals. Notably, the retail vertical showed
a significant uptick, with three major retailers selecting our platform
for their digital transformation initiatives. Canada contributed
$210K, up 17% from Q1.

EMEA:
Revenue of $1.17M, representing 23% of total. Performance improved
from Q1 as currency headwinds stabilized and the EMEA team closed
several large deals that had slipped from the prior quarter. The UK
remained the strongest individual market at $385K, with Germany at
$275K. The Nordics showed improvement with a $120K deal in Sweden
anchored by a major telecommunications company.

APAC:
Revenue of $620K, representing 12% of total. The region showed strong
growth of 19% quarter-over-quarter. Japan was the standout performer
at $225K, driven by a partnership with a local systems integrator that
generated three enterprise deals. Australia contributed $195K, flat
with Q1 but with a growing pipeline entering Q3.

Latin America:
Revenue of $250K, representing 5% of total. The region maintained its
growth trajectory with Brazil contributing $155K. Mexico emerged as a
notable growth market with $45K in Q2 revenue and a promising pipeline
for Q3. The channel partner strategy is beginning to scale, with two
new partners signed during the quarter.
